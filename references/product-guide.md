# Evaluation and integration scope

This public package is experimental. It supplies an advisory procedure and selected offline consistency checks.
It does not supply a hosted assessment service, model invocation, independent evidence verifier, production adapter,
service-level commitment, or certification. Any offered engagement needs a separately agreed scope and authority.

For evaluation, use synthetic inventory and the bundled tests. Real integration requires a private workspace,
a reviewed data-flow design, trusted scanner acquisition, explicit authorization for any AI-provider submission,
and controls over logs, artifacts, retention, and access. A Git archive is not a secret scrubber. A read-only
sandbox does not establish confidentiality of everything readable by its operating-system identity.

Treat the deployment contract as requirements for future implementation. Review candidate binding, evidence
freshness, independently observed rehearsal, approval authenticity, restore, canary isolation, rollback monitoring,
and final readback before relying on any production process. The bundled `readiness` command is intentionally disabled.

Report exactly what was examined and executed. A passing fixture test is not customer compatibility or a working
production recovery path. Keep commercial pricing, guarantees, support terms, and licensing decisions outside
technical claims until separately approved by the repository owner.
